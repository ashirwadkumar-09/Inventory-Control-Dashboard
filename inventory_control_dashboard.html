Model logic: EOQ = √(2DS/H), where D = annual demand, S = ordering cost/order, H = carrying cost/unit/year. Continuous-review reorder point = average daily demand × lead time + safety stock. Periodic-review reorder point = average demand × (lead time + review period) + safety stock. Safety stock uses z × daily demand standard deviation × √lead time. Total annual inventory cost = (D/Q)S + (Q/2)H. ABC classification ranks items by annual consumption value (D × unit cost) and applies the editable cumulative cutoffs above. This is a planning model; actual policy may also account for MOQ, supplier constraints, seasonality and review-specific demand variability.
